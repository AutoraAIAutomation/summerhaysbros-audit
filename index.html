04 · The launch structure

How we'd launch it.

Six phases. Each one builds the belief needed for the next. The close at the end must be real; reopening destroys credibility for the next launch.

  1. 1

    Build up

    Engage the community and tease that something big is coming. Interactive stories, polls, Q&A boxes and DMs touching their pain points and showing your results.

  2. 2

    Invitation

    Announce it's on the way and invite the audience into a private WhatsApp or Telegram group where the details will drop. Tease a time-limited bonus.

  3. 3

    Anticipation

    Build curiosity inside the group. Share the results people can achieve and make the timeline crystal clear so everyone is ready the moment doors open.

  4. 4

    Reveal

    The full breakdown: what's included, the exclusive bonus, the price and the open/close dates. Leave no doubt about what's on the table.

  5. 5

    Conversion

    Send the enrolment link, run a poll to see who's in or has questions, and follow up one-to-one in the DMs with everyone who raises a hand. This is where most sales happen.

  6. 6

    Urgency

    Countdown messages at 24h, 12h, 6h, 2h and closed. Make them picture exactly what they lose by staying out. Then close for real.

Why this order

Nothing is sold until the audience has raised a hand three times: in stories, in the group and in the DMs. By the time the link goes out, the people buying have already decided.
